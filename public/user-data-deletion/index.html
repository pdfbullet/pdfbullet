User Data Deletion Request

At Pdf Bullet, we prioritize your privacy and provide full control over your personal data. If you wish to delete your account and all associated information, please use one of the methods below.

Note: Deleting your account is permanent. All your files, configurations, and personal data will be wiped from our systems and cannot be recovered.

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)

The fastest and most secure way to delete your data is through your account dashboard. This verifies your identity instantly.

  1. Log in to your Pdf Bullet account.
  2. Go to Account Settings.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section at the bottom.
  4. Click the "Delete My Account" button.
  5. Confirm your decision in the final prompt.

Option 2: Email Request

If you cannot access your account, you can submit a manual deletion request.

Send an email to: Support@pdfbullet.com

Subject: Data Deletion Request

Please include the following in your email:

  • Your registered email address.
  • A brief statement confirming you want your account and all data deleted.

We will process manual requests within 7 business days after identity verification.
